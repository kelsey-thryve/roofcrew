Glenfield questions

How do I know if my roof needs replacing?

Look for leaks that keep coming back, rust, lifting sheets or cracked tiles. If it's the original roof, it's usually near the end of its life.

Do you cover the rest of the North Shore?

Yes. We work right across the Shore, from Albany to Devonport.
