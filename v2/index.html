Questions people ask us

How do I know if my roof needs replacing?

If leaks keep coming back after repairs, the roof is 30 or more years old, or you can see rust, lifting sheets, cracked tiles or water stains on your ceilings, it is usually time to look at a new roof. Mike will check it on a free site visit and tell you straight whether a repair will do.

How much does a reroof cost in Auckland?

It depends on the size and shape of your roof, access, and the material you choose. The ranges above are a rough guide for small, standard and large homes. After a free site visit you get a fixed, itemised price, so there are no surprises. [Mike to confirm ranges]

Is it worth repairing my roof, or should I replace it?

A repair makes sense for a one-off problem on a roof that is otherwise sound. If you are patching the same roof every winter, the money usually goes further on a new roof with a fresh guarantee. We will tell you honestly which one you need.
