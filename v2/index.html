Questions people ask us

How do I know if my roof needs replacing?

If leaks keep coming back after repairs, the roof is 30 or more years old, or you can see rust, lifting sheets, cracked tiles or water stains on your ceilings, it is usually time to look at a new roof. Mike will check it on a free site visit and tell you straight whether a repair will do.

How much does a reroof cost in Auckland?

It depends on the size and shape of your roof, access, and the material you choose. The ranges above are a rough guide for small, standard and large homes. After a free site visit you get a fixed, itemised price, so there are no surprises.

Is it worth repairing my roof, or should I replace it?

A repair makes sense for a one-off problem on a roof that is otherwise sound. If you are patching the same roof every winter, the money usually goes further on a new roof with a fresh guarantee. We will tell you honestly which one you need.

How long does a reroof take, and can we stay home?

It depends on the size of your roof, access and the weather. Mike gives you a timeframe with your fixed quote, so you know what to expect before we start. You can usually stay home while we work, and we tidy the site at the end of every day.

Do you offer finance?

Yes. Finance is available so you can spread the cost of a new roof. Just ask when you book your free quote.

Which roofing material is best for Auckland homes?

For most Auckland homes, COLORSTEEL® long-run is the popular choice: it's light, handles coastal wind and rain well, and comes in a wide range of colours. Pressed metal tile gives the look of tile with the strength of steel, while concrete tile offers good thermal and acoustic performance but is heavier. Mike brings samples to your free site visit.
