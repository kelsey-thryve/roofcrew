Our story

Family-owned, three decades in.

The Roof Crew is a family-owned business with over 30 years' experience in the building industry. We know what it's like to have that awful feeling of seeing a leak on your ceiling, and we pride ourselves on making sure you get a roof that's built to last and looks amazing.

Our crew are clean-cut, professional and extremely knowledgeable. We remove your old roof and dispose of it responsibly, work with safety netting and edge protection, and we're fully insured for your protection. We're also distributors and installers for Metrotile NZ, part of the RossRoof Group.
