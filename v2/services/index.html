What we do

Our services

Most requested

Reroofing

If your roof keeps leaking or has simply had its day, we'll take it off and start again. We strip the old roof, replace any rotten timber we find, and fit new underlay, flashings and your choice of roofing. It's all done under full scaffolding, and we take the old roof away.

See reroofing →

New roof installation

Building a new home or adding an extension? We work with homeowners, builders and developers, and help you choose the roof, fascia and spouting to suit the job.

Learn more →

Roof repairs

We fix leaks and damaged, slipped or cracked tiles. If a repair won't solve the problem, we'll tell you before you spend money on it.

Learn more →

Guttering and downpipes

We install new spouting and downpipes in colours that match your roof. It's worth doing with a reroof, when the scaffolding is already up.

Learn more →

Roof inspections and reports

Not sure what shape your roof is in? We'll check it and give you a written report with photos, which is handy before buying or selling.

Roofing options

Roofing materials

COLORSTEEL® roofing

This is the long-run steel you see on homes all over New Zealand, and it's our most popular option. There's a huge range of colours and profiles, including options made for coastal areas.

Learn more →

Metal tile roofing

Metal tiles give you the look of a tiled roof at a fraction of the weight. They're a popular way to replace an old tile roof without losing its character.

Learn more →

Concrete tile replacement

We specialise in replacing old concrete tile roofs with long-run COLORSTEEL® or metal tile. It takes weight off the house and gives you a modern roof.

Learn more →
