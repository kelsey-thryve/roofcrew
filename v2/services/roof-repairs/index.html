Roof repairs

Repair or replace?

A repair usually makes sense when the damage is in one spot and the roof has years left in it. If leaks are showing up in different places, or it's the home's original roof, a reroof may be better value.

Roof repair questions

Can you come out urgently?

No, we don't take on urgent leak or storm jobs, but we can usually visit within the next working day.

What should I do if my roof is leaking?

Put a bucket under the drip and move anything valuable out of the way. Don't get on the roof yourself, then call or text Mike on 021 903 822.
