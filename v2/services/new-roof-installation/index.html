Choosing your roof

Start with the roof type: COLORSTEEL® long-run or Gerard metal tile. Then choose your colour. We have samples, so you can see how it'll look before you commit.

New roof installation

Working with builders and developers

We roof new residential homes, specialising in long-run COLORSTEEL® and Gerard metal tile roofing systems. We fit in with the other trades on site and take site safety seriously, with safety netting and edge protection.

We cover the wider Auckland region, from Warkworth in the north to East and West Auckland, Franklin, Pukekohe and south of the Bombay Hills.

New build questions

When should I talk to you about a new build roof?

As early as you can, ideally when you have plans. That gives us time to advise on materials and fit your build schedule.

Can you roof an unusual design?

Yes. We just need to make sure it's done to the Building Code.
