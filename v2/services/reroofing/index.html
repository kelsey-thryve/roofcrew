Reroofing in Auckland

Reroofing means taking your old roof off and installing a new one on the same house. It's the right call when leaks keep coming back or the roof has reached the end of its life. We handle the whole job, from scaffolding to taking the old roof away.

How much does a reroof cost in Auckland?

A COLORSTEEL® reroof usually costs about $16,000 to $28,000 on a standard home. Roofs under 100m² are around $12,000 to $18,000, and large or complex roofs run from $25,000 to $40,000 or more.

These are rough ranges, not quotes. Pitch, access and what's under the old roof all affect the final price.

Reroofing questions

Do I need to move out during a reroof?

No, most people stay home, though it can be noisy.

Can you replace a tile roof with steel?

Yes. Plenty of people switch to a lighter roof. Mike will explain what's involved at your site visit.

What if you find rot under the old roof?

We'll show you what we've found and explain your options before going further. If it needs structural timber work, we'll bring in a builder we trust.
