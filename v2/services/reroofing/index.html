What's included

Same house. A completely new roof.

We strip your old roof, dispose of it responsibly and install new underlay, flashings, ridge capping and your choice of roofing, all under full scaffolding.

  • Old roof stripped and disposed of responsibly
  • New underlay, flashings and ridge capping
  • Rotten battens or purlins found and replaced
  • Full scaffolding and edge protection
  • Fully guaranteed workmanship and product
  • Finance available
When you need it

Signs it's time for a new roof

  • Leaks keep coming back after repairs
  • Roof is 30+ years old
  • Water stains on your ceilings
  • Rust, lifting sheets or cracked tiles
  • Faded, chalky or mossy surface

How it works

How a reroof works

1. Free site visit

We measure up and check what's underneath, usually within 3 working days.

2. Clean install

Scaffold up, old roof off, new roof on, with a tidy site at the end of every day.

3. Sign-off and guarantee

A final check together, then your workmanship and product guarantees.
