The Roof Crew Limited: Privacy Policy

What we collect: When you fill in one of our forms, we collect your name, phone number, email address and your answers about your roof.

Why we collect it: To contact you about your enquiry, arrange an inspection and provide a quote. We don’t use it for anything else.

Who sees it: Only The Roof Crew team. We don’t sell or share your details with anyone else.

How long we keep it: Only as long as we need it for your enquiry or job, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Your rights: Under the Privacy Act 2020, you can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted.
