Our story

Family-owned. Auckland through and through.

The Roof Crew is a family-owned roofing business led by Mike Church. For more than 30 years we've been reroofing, building and repairing roofs right across Auckland — from lifestyle blocks in Pukekohe to character homes on the North Shore.

We've kept things simple: an honest price up front, a clean-cut crew on site, and a roof that's sorted before the next storm. It's why so much of our work comes from neighbours and referrals.

  • 1000+ roofs completed across Auckland
  • RANZ (Roofing Association of NZ) member
  • Every job fully guaranteed — workmanship and product
  • Finance available to spread the cost
